Before You Buy

Terms & care notes for your little bloom ♡

💡 What our lamps are for

  • Our flower lamps are decorative mood lamps: lovely as room décor, a cosy study-desk or bedside lamp, and soft extra lighting.
  • They give a warm, gentle glow. They are not meant to replace your main room light or a bright task lamp.

🌷 Handmade, so every piece is unique

  • Every lamp is handmade petal by petal, so shape, size and small details may differ slightly from the photos.
  • Colours can look a little different in person because of screens and lighting.

🔋 Power & batteries

  • LED lamps run on small button cell batteries. USB lamps need a USB plug (a phone charger adapter or power bank).
  • Batteries are consumables. Replacements can be bought at DIY stores, at your own cost.
  • Remove the batteries if you won’t use the lamp for a long time.
  • Switch the lamp off when you leave the room or go to sleep.

⚠️ Care & safety

  • Keep away from water, damp places and direct sunlight (colours may fade).
  • Keep away from heat, candles and open flames.
  • Not a toy. Keep away from small children and pets, because of wire ends and small parts.
  • To clean, dust gently with a soft brush or a hairdryer on the cool setting.

💳 Orders & payment

  • Full payment is required for every order, including self pickup. Please send your receipt on WhatsApp after paying.
  • Pre-order lamps are made to order. The postage date shown on the website is our target date.
  • Because each lamp is handmade for you, orders can’t be cancelled or refunded once payment is made.

📦 Postage & pickup

  • We pack every lamp carefully, but courier delays are outside our control.
  • Please record an unboxing video. If your lamp arrives damaged, WhatsApp us within 24 hours with the video so we can help.
  • Self pickup is at Taman Sentosa, Klang. We’ll WhatsApp you the exact spot and time.
